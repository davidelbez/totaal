# Privacy policy — Totaal

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Totaal is a Claude plugin made of instructions (skills) and readable scripts. It has no server, no account and no analytics of its own. The authors of Totaal receive no data from anyone who uses it.

## What it reads
When you ask Claude to, Totaal reads your company's tools through the connectors you choose to connect (for example email, calendar, CRM, chat, meeting recorder), with your own permissions. This can include personal data such as the names, roles and email addresses of clients and colleagues.

## What it stores, and where
Totaal writes what the team learns into a company wiki: Markdown files in a private Git repository that your company creates and owns, usually on GitHub. That can include contact pages (name, role, organisation, work email) and summaries of exchanges. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

By rule, the wiki never stores: personal data about colleagues beyond their role, HR matters, health information, named performance evaluations, salaries, bank details or secret values such as passwords and API keys.

## What it sends
Its only network traffic is git (fetch, commit, push) to your company's own wiki repository. Its scripts make no other network calls and send no telemetry.

## Retention and deletion
Your company decides how long the wiki keeps anything. Delete a page or the whole repository, and the data is gone from Totaal's side; Totaal keeps no copy.

## Your choices
You choose which connectors to connect, who can access the repository, and what the wiki may contain (see the hard-rules page created at setup). Claude never sends, signs, pays or publishes on anyone's behalf without that person's go-ahead on the final version.
