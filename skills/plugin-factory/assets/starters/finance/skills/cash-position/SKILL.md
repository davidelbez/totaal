# Cash position

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- ~~accounting balances and receivables (live)
- ~~CRM deals expected to close, recurring costs

## Steps
1. Read balances and receivables live; state the date and scope.
2. List expected receipts and payments for the coming weeks.
3. Give a range, not a single number, when inputs are uncertain.

## Output
A dated summary with sources and assumptions.

## Rules
- A forecast is a range with its assumptions. → [[how-we-work]]
