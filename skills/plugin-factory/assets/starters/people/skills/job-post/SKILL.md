# Job post

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the closest past job post
- the role's scope from the team page and the manager

## Steps
1. Start from the closest past post.
2. Describe the real work of the first 6 months, the team, what success looks like.
3. List must-haves separately from nice-to-haves; keep it short.

## Output
Job post draft.

## Rules
- Inclusive, plain language; no internal jargon. → [[how-we-work]]
