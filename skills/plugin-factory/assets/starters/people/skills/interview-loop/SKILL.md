# Interview loop

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the job post and the role's must-haves
- interviewers' calendars

## Steps
1. Define stages and who runs each, with what they assess.
2. Write questions per stage tied to the must-haves.
3. Propose slots in ~~calendar; create events only on go-ahead.

## Output
Loop page + interviewer briefs + proposed slots.

- Candidate evaluations never go into the wiki. → [[hard-rules]]
