# Supplier follow up

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- supplier pages and contracts
- ~~email threads with the supplier

## Steps
1. List what is pending with each supplier and since when.
2. Draft one short follow-up per supplier with the one thing needed.
3. Record deadlines and renewal dates on the supplier page.

## Output
Follow-up drafts + updated supplier pages.

## Rules
- One request per message. → [[how-we-work]]
- Never commit the company to anything without the owner's go-ahead. → [[hard-rules]]
