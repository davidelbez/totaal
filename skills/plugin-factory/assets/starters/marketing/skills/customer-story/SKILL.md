# Customer story

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- account journal, reports, transcripts
- the client's approval status

## Steps
1. Pick the story arc from the journal: context, action, result.
2. Use results with their source and date; no inflated figures.
3. Prepare it for client approval before any publication.

## Output
Story draft + approval request draft.

## Rules
- Never publish a client story without written approval. → [[hard-rules]]
