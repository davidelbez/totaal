# Campaign brief

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- past campaigns and their results (read live)
- audience lists, hard rules on naming and figures

## Steps
1. Start from the past campaign closest to this goal and its results.
2. Choose the channel first: it decides the result more than the text.
3. Write the brief: goal, audience, channel, message, timing, how it is measured.
4. Plan a test before the full send.

## Output
Brief page in the wiki + draft messages.

## Rules
- The channel decides the result, not the text. → [[how-we-work]]
- Customer-facing names and approved figures only. → [[hard-rules]]
