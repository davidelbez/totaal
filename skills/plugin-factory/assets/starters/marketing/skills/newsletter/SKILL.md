# Newsletter

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- last newsletter (structure and tone)
- releases, account stories cleared for publication, events

## Steps
1. List candidate items since the last issue; pick the strongest 3–5.
2. Write each item: what it is, why it matters to the reader, one link.
3. Prepare the draft in ~~email marketing; never schedule without go-ahead.

## Output
Newsletter draft.
