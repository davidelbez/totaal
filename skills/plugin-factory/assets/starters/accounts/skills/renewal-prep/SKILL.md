# Renewal prep

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the card (contract end and renewal decision dates are distinct)
- journal, reports sent, open issues, opportunities section

## Steps
1. Check both dates; if the renewal decision date is unknown, find out who decides and when.
2. Summarise value delivered with sources, risks, and open issues.
3. List expansion options already signalled by the client.
4. Write the plan: who to meet, what to propose, when.

## Output
Renewal page in the account folder + recommended tasks.

## Rules
- Contract end and renewal decision are two dates. → [[accounts]]
- Expansion ideas come from client signals, dated and sourced. → [[accounts]]
