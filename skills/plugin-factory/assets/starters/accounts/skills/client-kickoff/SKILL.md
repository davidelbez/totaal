# Client kickoff

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the signed proposal and the sales journal
- the last kickoff of a similar client (start from it)

## Steps
1. Move or create the account folder in `active/`; fill the card.
2. Draft the kickoff agenda and the list of information to collect.
3. Write the launch plan with dates and owners on both sides.
4. Prepare the meeting page; after the meeting, fill the minutes.

## Output
Account card + kickoff page + launch plan.

## Rules
- Start from the closest past kickoff. → [[accounts]]
- Every action has an owner and a date. → [[how-we-work]]
