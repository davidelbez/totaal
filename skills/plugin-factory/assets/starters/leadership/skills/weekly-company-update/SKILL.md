# Weekly company update

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- `wiki/hot.md`, this week's digests and log entries
- account journals touched this week, renewals within 90 days
- ~~CRM pipeline changes, ~~BI headline numbers (read live, dated)
- decisions pages dated this week

## Steps
1. Gather the week's facts by source; count before concluding.
2. Pick the 3 things that matter most; one line each with its link.
3. List risks with owner and next step, decisions taken, and what comes next week.
4. Draft in the team's tone; show it before sending in ~~chat or ~~email.

## Output
Title naming the week · Top 3 · Accounts (moves, risks) · Decisions · Next week · Sources and dates.

## Rules
- Numbers come from the source with their date and scope, never copied from a page. → [[hard-rules]]
- No judgement of people; facts and owners only. → [[how-we-work]]
