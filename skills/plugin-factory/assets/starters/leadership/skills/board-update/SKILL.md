# Board update

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- last update sent (start from it)
- ~~BI and finance figures, read live and dated
- wiki: decisions, wins, losses, hires of the period

## Steps
1. Start from the previous update's structure.
2. Fill highlights, numbers (source and date on each), risks, and asks.
3. Mark every figure that could not be verified.
4. Present the draft; never send.

## Output
Highlights · Key numbers (dated) · Wins and losses · Risks · Asks.

## Rules
- Only figures cleared for external use. → [[hard-rules]]
- Never send without the lead's go-ahead on the final text. → [[hard-rules]]
