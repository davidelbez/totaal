# Feature spec

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- product-feedback lines on the topic
- decisions and past specs

## Steps
1. Gather the evidence: who asked, how often, in their words.
2. Write problem, users, scope in and out, success measure, open questions.
3. Propose the tickets; create them only after validation.

## Output
Spec page + proposed tickets.

## Rules
- Each requirement traces to feedback or a decision. → [[product-feedback]]
