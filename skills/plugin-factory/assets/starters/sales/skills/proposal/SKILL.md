# Proposal

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the 2 closest past proposals (shared drive)
- the client's needs from the journal and transcripts
- price rules and hard rules

## Steps
1. Find the closest past proposal and start from it.
2. Adapt scope, timeline and price strictly from the price rules; mark anything unsure.
3. Reread what was already sent to this client before quoting.
4. File the draft in the account folder and list it in Key documents.

## Output
Proposal document + a cover mail draft.

## Rules
- Never invent a price: use the rules or ask. → [[hard-rules]]
- Reread past sends to this client before quoting. → [[accounts]]
