# Meeting follow up

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the meeting transcript (never the AI recap)
- the prep page and the account journal

## Steps
1. Digest the transcript (meeting-digest method): decisions, actions, owners, dates.
2. Draft the follow-up: thanks, what we understood, agreed next steps with dates, documents attached.
3. Move the deal stage only on a clear signal; set a dated next action.
4. Show the draft; send only on go-ahead.

## Output
A short client mail (one request at most) + journal line + deal next action.

## Rules
- One request per mail, two at most. → [[how-we-work]]
- No internal jargon or tool names to clients. → [[hard-rules]]
