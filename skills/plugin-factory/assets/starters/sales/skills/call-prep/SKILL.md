# Call prep

> Starter skill from Totaal, adapted to {{ORG}} at setup. It is meant to change: every correction and every new habit of the team becomes a rule here (evening tuning).

## Inputs
- the account folder (card, journal, contacts) or a new prospect folder
- ~~CRM deal and stage, ~~email threads, past meetings

## Steps
1. Open or create the account folder; read the card and the last journal lines.
2. List what is already known and decided, then the open questions to ask.
3. Write the prep page in `meetings/` (goal, agenda, talking points, questions).
4. Note what proof points and offers fit this prospect.

## Output
Prep page with goal, known facts, questions to ask, proof points, next step to propose.

## Rules
- Never present a settled topic as open: read the journal first. → [[accounts]]
- Proof points come only from the approved list. → [[hard-rules]]
