# First-week quests

> Learning by doing. The newcomer asks Claude each question. Claude answers **from the wiki, with the pages and dates it used**. If the wiki cannot answer, Claude says so, logs the question in [[onboarding-gaps]], and asks who knows. Add company-specific quests below the generic ones.

## Generic quests
1. What does {{ORG}} sell, to whom, and why do clients choose us? (expected: [[how-we-work]])
2. Which rules must I never break with a client? ([[hard-rules]])
3. Who decides what in the team, and who do I ask for what? ([[who-is-who]])
4. What are our five most important accounts right now, and what is the risk on each?
5. What happened with <one of my accounts> in the last 30 days? (journal, newest source date)
6. Which meetings do I have this week, and what is already decided for each?
7. Where are the files of <account>, and which are the key documents?
8. What did the team ship or decide last week? (`hot.md`, digests)
9. How do I ask Claude to do X without watching it? (the morning report, recommended tasks)
10. Ask Claude to record a real event of today in the right account journal, and check it is saved in the wiki.
