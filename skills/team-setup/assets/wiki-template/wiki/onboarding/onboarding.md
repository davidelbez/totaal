# Onboarding at {{ORG}}

> Goal: a newcomer ships a real, autonomous piece of work in their first week and is fully autonomous in 30 days — without colleagues repeating what the wiki already knows.
> Run it with the plugin skill `onboarding`: "prepare the arrival of <name>", "start my onboarding", "onboarding check-in".

## What we know
- The wiki answers first, people answer second. Every question the wiki could not answer becomes a line in [[onboarding-gaps]] and, within the week, a page. The newcomer is the best tester of the wiki.
- Onboarding has two halves, run together: **joining the company** (people, accounts, how we work) and **joining the system** (Claude, connectors, plugin, routines).
- Before day 1, the manager prepares everything: the newcomer page, the buddy, the personal reading path, the accesses, the first-week calendar, and three scheduled check-ins (day 1, day 7, day 30).
- Day 1 ends with: Totaal and their personal company plugins installed and enabled, every connector of the role connected, the wiki attached, a preferences page, and the morning routine scheduled.
- Week 1 is learned by doing: the quests in [[first-week-quests]], each answered from the wiki with its sources, plus the newcomer's first "three writes".
- Day 30 ends with a review: the path milestones, the gap list closed, and what to change in this onboarding for the next person.
- Measures: days to the first autonomous deliverable; share of the newcomer's questions answered by the wiki; gaps closed.
- When someone leaves: their accounts get a new owner in the card, their personal Claude memory is reviewed and anything durable moved to the wiki, their scheduled tasks are stopped, their accesses are removed.
