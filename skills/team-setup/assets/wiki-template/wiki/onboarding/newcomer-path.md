# Newcomer path — 30 days

> Template. The `onboarding` skill copies it into the newcomer's own page (`entities/team/<name>.md`, section `## Onboarding`) and adapts it to the role. Each line is a checkbox the check-ins read.

## Before day 1 — the manager (with Claude)
- [ ] Newcomer page created from the person template: role, scope, manager, buddy, start date.
- [ ] Buddy named (someone who is not the manager).
- [ ] Accesses requested for every tool of the role ([[tools-and-access]]), owner and date for each.
- [ ] Personal reading path generated: the domain summaries of the role, the 5 accounts they will touch, [[who-is-who]], [[glossary]], [[hard-rules]].
- [ ] First-week calendar: a 30-minute intro with each teammate, the team rituals, two shadowed external meetings.
- [ ] Welcome message drafted for the manager to send.
- [ ] Check-ins scheduled: day 1 evening, day 7, day 30 ([[onboarding-checkins]]).

## Day 1 — joining the system (with Claude, ~1 hour)
- [ ] Totaal is installed **and enabled**; GitHub is connected (one time; Claude does the rest).
- [ ] My personal company plugins are installed and enabled (they will evolve with my use).
- [ ] Every connector of the role is connected (each one with the reason it matters) — or the refusal is written down.
- [ ] Claude can open the company wiki for me and tell me the state of the company and of my accounts.
- [ ] Preferences page created (`wiki/preferences/<name>.md`): report format, schedule, modules.
- [ ] Morning routine scheduled as a cloud task (a pointer prompt). Tuning scheduled if the role patches skills.
- [ ] First question asked to the wiki, answered with sources.

## Week 1 — joining the company
- [ ] The quests of [[first-week-quests]] done; every unanswered question logged in [[onboarding-gaps]].
- [ ] Intros held; one line per person added to their page under "How to work with them" if useful.
- [ ] Two meetings shadowed and digested (transcript, not recap).
- [ ] First three writes done: a dated note, a journal line, a rule proposed for a skill.
- [ ] First autonomous deliverable shipped (the manager names it on day 1).

## Days 8–30 — autonomy
- [ ] Owns their accounts or topics; morning report read daily.
- [ ] Has validated or refused recommended tasks every day.
- [ ] Has closed at least five gaps of [[onboarding-gaps]] (or had them closed).
- [ ] Day-30 review held: milestones, what slowed them down, what to change in this path.
