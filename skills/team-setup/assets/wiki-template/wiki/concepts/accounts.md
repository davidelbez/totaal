# Accounts

## What we know
- Each account has a folder `entities/accounts/<status>/<slug>/` from its first mention: a card, a journal, contacts, meetings.
- Status folders: `active`, `prospects`, `partners`, `lost`. A folder moves when the status changes; `[[links]]` do not break.
- The journal is newest first, one line per event, never a copied KPI. See the `account-journal` skill.
- Contract end and renewal decision are two distinct dates on every card.
