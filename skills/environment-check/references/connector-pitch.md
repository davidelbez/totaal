# Why connect it — one line per category

Use these lines when proposing a connector. Name the person's own tool, not the category.

| Category | What connecting it unlocks |
|---|---|
| email | Every client exchange lands in the right account journal each morning, received and sent, without forwarding anything. |
| calendar | Tomorrow's client meetings get a prep page with what is already decided; meetings held get their minutes. |
| chat | Requests teammates made to you are tracked until done, including in group chats without a title. |
| CRM | Your tasks and deals show up in the morning report; deal stages move on clear signals; renewals are anticipated 90/60/30 days ahead. |
| task tracker | Recommended tasks become real tasks with one click; overdue items are surfaced with their age. |
| meeting recorder | Every call is digested from its transcript — decisions, actions, risks — and filed with the account, with nothing lost. |
| ticketing | Support trends in your users' own words; product feedback logged once, never twice. |
| shared drive | Contracts, decks and exports are found and filed in the right account folder, and listed as key documents. |
| BI / product analytics | Numbers come from the source, dated, with their scope — never copied stale from a page. |
| e-signature, accounting, email marketing | The system of record is checked before anyone says "sent", "signed" or "paid". |

No connector exists for a tool → say which rung replaces it (API key, or the browser) and what that costs: browser steps need your computer awake.
