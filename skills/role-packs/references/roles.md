# Role packs

Each pack: what Claude takes off the plate · morning-report focus · connectors to push · the base plugin that serves the role and its starter skills. Base plugins are starting points: they grow and change with use.

## Leadership (founder, CEO, team lead)
- **Off the plate**: weekly state of the company from the wiki and the tools; decision pages written from a discussion; board or investor update drafts; preparation of the team review; follow-up of every decision with owner and deadline.
- **Morning focus**: decisions waiting, accounts at risk, renewals within 90 days, team blockers, what shipped.
- **Connectors**: email, calendar, chat, CRM, shared drive, BI.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-leadership`: weekly-company-update · decision-memo · board-update (starters; more skills grow from use).

## Sales and business development
- **Off the plate**: every prospect gets a folder and a journal from the first mail; call prep from everything already known; follow-up drafts after each meeting; deal stages moved on clear signals; quotes and proposals started from past ones.
- **Morning focus**: deals without a dated next action, prospects who replied, meetings tomorrow with prep, proposals waiting on an answer.
- **Connectors**: email, calendar, CRM, meeting recorder, e-signature.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-sales`: call-prep · meeting-follow-up · proposal (starters; more skills grow from use).

## Account management and customer success
- **Off the plate**: account journals kept up to date from mail and calls; renewal cadence 90/60/30 days before contract end; client reports drafted from the numbers; expansion signals spotted and filed; meeting minutes with actions.
- **Morning focus**: accounts touched since yesterday, renewals coming, unanswered client requests, health signals.
- **Connectors**: email, calendar, CRM, meeting recorder, ticketing, BI.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-accounts`: client-report · renewal-prep · client-kickoff (starters; more skills grow from use).

## Customer support
- **Off the plate**: reply drafts in the tone of past answers, left as drafts; each requester checked against the records; recurring issues logged once in the product-feedback register; weekly trends in users' own words.
- **Morning focus**: open tickets by age, tickets waiting on the team, new recurring issues.
- **Connectors**: ticketing, email, chat, the product's admin or database (read-only).
- **Base plugin** `<org>-support`: support-reply · bug-report (starters; more skills grow from use).

## Operations and administration
- **Off the plate**: files attached to mails filed in the right account folder and listed as key documents; recurring admin checklists; supplier and contract follow-up; scheduling; data cleanup in the tools.
- **Morning focus**: deadlines this week, documents received and not filed, admin tasks overdue.
- **Connectors**: email, calendar, shared drive, task tracker, e-signature.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-operations`: file-document · supplier-follow-up · monthly-admin (starters; more skills grow from use).

## Finance
- **Off the plate**: invoice and payment follow-up drafts; monthly close checklist; reconciliation helpers; budget versus actual summaries from the accounting tool; numbers always read live, dated, with their scope.
- **Morning focus**: invoices due or overdue, payments received, anomalies.
- **Connectors**: accounting, email, shared drive, CRM (for contract dates), e-signature.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-finance`: payment-reminder · monthly-close · cash-position (starters; more skills grow from use).
- **Keep out of the wiki**: amounts per person, salaries, bank details.

## Marketing and communications
- **Off the plate**: campaign drafts from past winners; content calendar; customer stories built from account journals (with the client's approval); performance read from the source and dated; messaging kept consistent with the hard rules.
- **Morning focus**: campaigns sending this week, results of the last ones, content due.
- **Connectors**: email marketing, social or CMS tools, BI or analytics, shared drive.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-marketing`: campaign-brief · newsletter · customer-story (starters; more skills grow from use).

## People and HR
- **Off the plate**: onboarding run end to end (the `onboarding` skill); job posts from past ones; interview scheduling; policy pages kept in the wiki; offboarding checklist.
- **Morning focus**: arrivals and departures coming, onboarding check-ins, open roles.
- **Connectors**: email, calendar, chat, the HR tool, shared drive.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-people`: job-post · interview-loop (starters; more skills grow from use).
- **Keep out of the wiki**: personal data, health, evaluations, disputes.

## Product and engineering
- **Off the plate**: product feedback from every channel consolidated weekly; specs drafted from feedback and decisions; release notes in plain language for the rest of the team; incidents traced; bug reports turned into tickets after validation.
- **Morning focus**: new feedback by severity, what shipped, incidents, tickets waiting on a decision.
- **Connectors**: task tracker or issue tracker, code host, error tracking, product analytics, ticketing.
- **Base plugin** `<org>-product`: feature-spec · release-notes · incident-review (starters; more skills grow from use).
